# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository is intended to be a respectful public collaboration space.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- focus on the work and the repository contents
- give constructive feedback
- respect privacy and avoid sharing non-public information about other people

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in this repository or related project spaces:

- harassment, threats, or intimidation
- hateful or discriminatory language or conduct
- personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
- publishing someone else's private information without permission
- posting secrets, credentials, or other non-public material

## Maintainer actions

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content, and may limit participation, when they believe it is necessary to protect the repository or its participants.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct concerns through a non-public channel to the repository maintainer. Do not use public issues for reports that contain private or sensitive information.

## Scope

This document applies to repository interactions such as issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments.
